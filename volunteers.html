PIROAKE Games Night Series — The Gambia

🎮 PIROAKE Games Night Series

Join the Volunteer Team

Are you a young person passionate about gaming, community engagement, and creating fun, inclusive experiences? We are looking for enthusiastic volunteers to support the PIROAKE Games Night Series in The Gambia!

Volunteers will have the opportunity to support the planning and delivery of our games nights, engage with participants, and contribute to building an exciting and welcoming community.

If you are interested in volunteering with us, we would love to hear from you!

📍 Location: The Gambia (Applicants must be resident in The Gambia) 📢 Please share this opportunity with anyone who may be interested.
