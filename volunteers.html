PIROAKE Games Night Series — The Gambia

PIROAKE Games Night Series

Join the Volunteer Team

Are you a young person passionate about curating events, gaming, community engagement, and creating fun, inclusive experiences?

WalkingFish is looking for enthusiastic volunteers in The Gambia to join us in bringing our activities and Games Night Series to life!

As a volunteer, you'll have the opportunity to:

  • Support the planning and delivery of events
  • Help create exciting gaming experiences
  • Engage with participants and build community
  • Contribute ideas and bring your creativity to the table
  • Be part of creating fun, welcoming experiences for everyone

Whether you're an aspiring event planner, gamer, creative, community builder, or simply someone who loves bringing people together, we want you on the team.

Come build, create, play and grow with us.

Location: The Gambia (Applicants must be resident in The Gambia) Please share this opportunity with anyone who may be interested.
